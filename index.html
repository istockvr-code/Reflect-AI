Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version: Audio never leaves your device. Only text transcripts are sent to our servers for analysis. We never sell your data. You can delete everything at any time.

1. Who We Are

Reflect ("we," "us," "our") provides a household wellness coaching service through the Reflect mobile application. We are not a medical provider. Reflect is not a substitute for professional therapy, counseling, or mental health treatment.

2. What We Collect

Audio — Stays On Your Device

The Reflect app captures audio through your device's microphone while monitoring is active. Raw audio is processed entirely on your device using on-device AI models and is never transmitted to our servers. Audio is discarded immediately after transcription.

Text Transcripts

After on-device transcription, text transcripts (not audio) are sent to our servers for communication pattern analysis. Transcripts are:

  • Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3)
  • Stored in encrypted form on our servers
  • Retained for a maximum of 90 days
  • Never sold to third parties
  • Never used to train AI models

Account Information

Email address, subscription status, and device registration information.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Analyze communication patterns to generate personalized growth insights
  • Deliver daily and weekly coaching recommendations
  • Maintain your subscription and account
  • Send push notifications with your insights

5. Not a Substitute for Professional Care

Reflect AI is not a therapist, counselor, or medical provider, and does not provide therapy, counseling, medical, or mental-health treatment of any kind. It is a self-reflection and communication wellness tool offered for general informational and educational purposes only. Its insights are generated by AI, are not professional advice, and should never be relied upon as a substitute for the guidance, diagnosis, or treatment of a qualified professional.

If you are genuinely struggling with your mental health or a mental illness, please seek help from a licensed professional. If you are in crisis or having thoughts of harming yourself, help is available 24/7:

Call or text 988 — the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (U.S.).

6. Children's Privacy

Reflect is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 as account holders. Children in the household may be incidentally recorded under the consent of their parent or legal guardian who has accepted this Privacy Policy.

7. Data Retention

  • Text transcripts: deleted after 90 days
  • Pattern summaries (compressed history): retained while account is active
  • Account data: deleted within 30 days of account deletion request

8. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Export your data — download all transcripts and insights as JSON via Settings → Export My Data
  • Delete your account — permanently deletes all data, transcripts, and insights via Settings → Delete Account
  • Pause monitoring — one tap from the home screen or notification bar

If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have additional rights under GDPR/UK GDPR including the right to access, rectification, erasure, and portability. Contact us at reflectaisupport@gmail.com.

9. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — text transcripts are sent to Anthropic's API for analysis. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and push notifications. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

We do not use advertising networks. We do not share your data with data brokers.

10. Crisis Detection

Reflect includes automated detection of language that may indicate a mental health or safety crisis. If such language is detected, the app will surface crisis support resources including the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and the National Domestic Violence Hotline. Crisis flags are logged internally (without transcript content) for safety audit purposes.

11. Security

  • Local database encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256)
  • All network communication encrypted with TLS 1.3
  • Server-side data encrypted at rest
  • Firebase Security Rules prevent cross-account data access

12. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via push notification and by updating this page. Continued use of Reflect after changes constitutes acceptance.
